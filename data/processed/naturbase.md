# Naturbase NiN naturtyper - the red-list suppression set

Generated by `scripts/fetch_naturbase.py`. Not a model feature and never becomes one.

`data\interim\naturbase_kristiansand_25833.geojson` - 3,702 polygons over Kristiansand kommune (post-2020 merge) plus a 2000 m margin, EPSG:25833.

CLAUDE.md requires the suppression capability in the tile pipeline from the start, not as a later policy layer. This is what it screens against. The argument for it is that a habitat suitability map is *most* accurate exactly where publishing it does most harm: the terrain that scores well for an interesting fungus is the terrain that shelters threatened ones.

## What counts as sensitive

The rule lives in `src/ingest/naturbase.py:is_sensitive` so it is one decision in one place. It is deliberately over-inclusive - suppressing an ordinary patch costs a slightly blurrier map, failing to suppress a threatened one costs the habitat. Any one trigger is enough:

* `UK_Truet` - Miljodirektoratet's own threatened-habitat-type flag
* `Lokalitetskvalitet >= 3` on a 0-4 scale
* `Naturmangfold >= 2` on a 0-2 scale

**1,783 of 3,702 polygons are sensitive** (48.2%), giving 1,857 rings to suppress around.

| trigger | polygons |
|---|---|
| threatened habitat type (UK_Truet) | 866 |
| high biodiversity (2/2) | 750 |
| high locality quality (3/4) | 155 |
| high locality quality (4/4) | 12 |

## What this does not cover

Most of Agder has never been surveyed for naturtyper. These polygons are where somebody looked, not where the sensitive habitat is. The unmapped ground is the far larger gap, and suppression cannot close it - it can only handle what Miljodirektoratet has recorded.

Localities the survey left unscored are treated as not sensitive. That is a gap rather than a judgement: unscored means nobody assessed it.

Neither mode is a security control. Both assume the sensitive polygons themselves are not published next to the suppressed map - and Naturbase is public, so a determined reader can recover the locations from the source regardless. Suppression raises the effort, it does not prevent.
