## What this is, and what it is not

**Not a model input.** The fitted model's rainfall feature is a 1991-2020 climatological mean for the fruiting window, which describes what a place is usually like. This file describes what the last three weeks were like, which is a different question and is not fitted to anything. No fungal record was used to derive or validate it.

## Why a percentile rather than millimetres

60 mm means different things in different places. A cell's total is compared against its own 30 years for the same calendar window, so the number that reaches the interface is 'wetter or drier than this place usually is by now', which is comparable across the map.
