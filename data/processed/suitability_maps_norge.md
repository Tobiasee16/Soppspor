# Suitability maps - Norway, 32 m

Generated by `scripts/build_maps_national.py` on 2026-09-14. 38,173 x 48,022 at 32 m, EPSG:25833, model `maxent-style (L1 logistic, linear + quadratic)`.

**These maps are not an edibility assessment and not a species identification.** They shade terrain by habitat plausibility from public geodata. A high cell means the terrain resembles places where the species has been recorded. It does not mean anything is there.

**Fitted on fourteen fylker** - everything outside Nordland fylke, minus a 4000 m buffer around the held-out points. That is the same model `transferability_norge.md` reports on, so the AUC quoted there describes the surface shipped here.

**Nordland fylke is the only part of this map that is a prediction onto ground the model never saw.** The other fourteen fylker are in-sample. Phase 0's Kristiansand map was held-out over its whole extent; this one is not, and the honest read is that the held-out number from Nordland is what bounds trust in the rest.
