# AR50 land cover, burned to the Agder grid

Generated by `scripts/build_ar50.py`. No model fitted.

`data\interim\ar50_agder_32m.tif` - uint8, 32 m, EPSG:25833, aligned cell-for-cell with `terrain_agder_32m.tif`.

## Why AR50 and not AR5

CLAUDE.md's data table asks for NIBIO AR5 at 1:5,000. Geonorge's catalogue reports FKB-AR5 as `AccessIsRestricted: true`, `DataAccess: "Norge digitalt begrenset"`, under Geovekst terms. That is a **licence** restriction, not a missing technical route: there is nothing to engineer around, and engineering around it would mean using data we are not licensed for. AR50 is the same agency's generalised product, published as open data under NLOD.

The cost is a factor of ten in scale. AR50's minimum mapping unit is far larger, so small clearings, narrow bogs and field edges are not in it. Read it as *roughly what kind of ground is this*, never as a boundary.

Reprojected EPSG:25832 -> EPSG:25833 on ingest. AR50 is offered for Agder only in 4258 and 25832, because Agder really does fall in UTM zone 32 - the same zone-32 trap CLAUDE.md's CRS note warns about, arriving from a different direction.

## The check that mattered

AR50 codes agriculture as `20`. AR5 uses `21`/`22`/`23` for agriculture and puts transport at `12`. Reading 20 the AR5 way would have relabelled every agricultural polygon in Agder as roads, and nothing would have failed. It was verified against the delivered data instead: every polygon with arealtype 20 carries a `jordbruk` code (24 fulldyrka, 25 innmarksbeite) and no other arealtype does.
