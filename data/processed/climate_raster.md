## Sensitivity to the window

FINDINGS section 6 claims the exact window matters little at
climatological scale. Testing it, against windows shifted 14 days:

| window | days | median (mm) | correlation with registered |
|---|---|---|---|
| registered | 221-293 | 342 | 1.0000 |
| earlier | 207-279 | 332 | 0.9977 |
| later | 235-307 | 374 | 0.9971 |

Lowest correlation 0.9971. The claim holds: the *spatial
pattern* is essentially the same whichever of these windows is
used, so the choice of window is not quietly doing the work. Note
what this does and does not say - the totals differ, but a model
reads the pattern, and the pattern is stable.
