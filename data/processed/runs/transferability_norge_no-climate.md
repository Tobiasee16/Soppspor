# Transferability - fitted on Norway minus Nordland, evaluated once on Nordland

Generated by `scripts/run_transfer.py` on 2026-09-14, tagged `no-climate`.

**Training extent:** Norway, mainland, minus the held-out region, minus the held-out extent and everything within 4000 m of it.
**Held-out extent:** Nordland fylke. Never seen during fitting.

This is the question CLAUDE.md's study-area section poses: does a model
fitted regionally hold up in an area it was not trained on? Every other
number in this project is internal to its own training region -
spatially blocked, but trained and tested inside one extent.

**It is not cross-validation.** There is one test set and one number per
model. The interval is a percentile bootstrap over the held-out points,
which covers sampling variation in those points and nothing else - in
particular it says nothing about whether Nordland's records are a
fair sample of Nordland, which on effort-biased data is the larger
worry.

## Null control

The synthetic null scored **0.500** on the held-out
extent, against a 0.60 ceiling. The transfer split
does not by itself manufacture signal.

## Predicted ordering, on the held-out extent

The same registered prediction, judged on transfer rather than on
blocked CV: *C. tubaeformis* best, *C. cibarius* middle, *A. muscaria*
worst.

* lightgbm (gradient-boosted trees): Craterellus tubaeformis > Amanita muscaria > Cantharellus cibarius - **FAIL**
* maxent-style (L1 logistic, linear + quadratic): Craterellus tubaeformis > Cantharellus cibarius > Amanita muscaria - **PASS**

Read this against the blocked-CV ordering in
[model_results.md](model_results.md), not instead of it. Agreement
between the two is the interesting case; disagreement means the blocked
figure did not survive being moved.
