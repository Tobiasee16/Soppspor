## Permutation importance, held-out block

Drop in AUC when each feature is shuffled in the test fold, against a
model fitted on the other four folds. Reported per model, because the
two need not agree and the AUC table above reports both.

This is also the check FINDINGS section 6 committed to: autumn
rainfall follows the same coast-inland gradient as the population, so
a model leaning on it may be reading accessibility rather than
ecology.

A caution about the null column. Its importances are near zero for
every feature, which is *not* evidence that the features are free of
effort bias. The null's presences are drawn from the same pool as the
background, so the two are environmentally identical by construction
and no feature can separate them. That column checks that the
pipeline does not leak, and nothing more.
